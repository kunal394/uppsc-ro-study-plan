Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q124 · PDF p26

Permanent Settlement is most directly associated with which region?

Answer: C. Bengal

Permanent Settlement was introduced in Bengal in 1793. Ryotwari is associated with major parts of Madras/Bombay; Mahalwari with parts of northern India.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q90 · PDF p19

Which of these places was annexed under Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse?

Answer: A. Jhansi

Jhansi was a Doctrine of Lapse annexation. Punjab and Awadh were acquired through different grounds/events; do not classify every British annexation as lapse.

किसी बात की इच्छा/आकांक्षा से रहित व्यक्ति के लिए उपयुक्त शब्द क्या है? Choose one Hindi word for a person free from desire or longing.

उत्तर: B. निःस्पृह

निःस्पृह का अर्थ इच्छा/स्पृहा से रहित है। निर्भीक भय का अभाव बताता है, जिज्ञासु जानने की इच्छा रखता है।

English explanation and option meanings

निःस्पृह means free from desire; निर्भीक means fearless, while जिज्ञासु expresses a wish to know. These are different states.

  • A. निर्भीक — Fearless
  • B. निःस्पृह — Free from desire
  • C. जिज्ञासु — Curious / eager to know
  • D. वाचाल — Talkative
