Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q55 · PDF p12

Articles 23 and 24 primarily protect which fundamental right?

Answer: D. Protection against exploitation

Article 23 prohibits trafficking and forced labour; Article 24 concerns child employment in specified hazardous settings. Articles 19–22 concern freedoms/protections, not this pair.

Which constitutional institution recommends how specified tax proceeds should be shared between the Union and states?

Answer: B. Finance Commission

The Finance Commission's Article 280 role includes recommendations on distribution of net tax proceeds and grants. It recommends; it does not itself legislate the tax.

The description 'quasi-federal' for India's constitutional system is associated with which scholar?

Answer: A. K. C. Wheare

K. C. Wheare used this description to emphasise the strong central features alongside federal arrangements. Do not confuse this attribution with a constitutional article.

Article 16 concerns equality of opportunity in public employment. Clause (2) lists prohibited grounds; Article 15 concerns a different general discrimination provision.

In which part of the Constitution are the provisions on elections grouped?

Answer: C. Part XV

Part XV covers elections, including the Election Commission framework. Fundamental Rights are in Part III; emergency provisions are in Part XVIII.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q143 · PDF p29

सर्वनाम-प्रयोग की दृष्टि से मानक रूप चुनें। Choose the standard formal form with correct pronoun usage.

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: B. मुझे यह पसंद नहीं।

यहाँ कर्म/अनुभवकर्ता के लिए ‘मुझे’ मानक रूप है। ‘मेरे को’ बोलचाल में मिल सकता है, पर इस औपचारिक व्याकरण-अभ्यास में ‘मुझे’ चुनें। वाक्य मूल प्रश्न से सरल करके लिखा गया है।

English explanation and option meanings

मुझे is the standard pronoun form for the experiencer in this construction. मेरे को occurs colloquially, but this exercise asks for formal usage. The common English meaning ‘I do not like this’ cannot establish which Hindi form is correct.

  • A. मेरे को यह पसंद नहीं। — Colloquial: I do not like this
  • B. मुझे यह पसंद नहीं। — Standard formal: I do not like this
  • C. मैं को यह पसंद नहीं। — Non-standard pronoun/case form
  • D. मेरी यह पसंद नहीं। — Non-standard construction

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: A. अनभिज्ञ

अभिज्ञ का अर्थ जानकार/परिचित है; अनभिज्ञ का अर्थ जानकारी न रखने वाला है। सर्वज्ञ और विज्ञ विपरीत अर्थ नहीं देते।

English explanation and option meanings

अभिज्ञ means informed or acquainted; अनभिज्ञ means unaware. सर्वज्ञ and विज्ञ do not express the opposite. Meanings are shown only after reveal so the gloss does not answer the question in advance.

  • A. अनभिज्ञ — Unaware/unacquainted
  • B. सर्वज्ञ — All-knowing
  • C. विज्ञ — Informed/knowledgeable
  • D. ज्ञानी — Learned/wise

उत्तर: C. सूक्ष्म–स्थूल

सूक्ष्म का विलोम स्थूल है। अर्थ का आकार/बारीकी वाला पक्ष देखें; कोमल और लघु समान विलोम नहीं हैं।
