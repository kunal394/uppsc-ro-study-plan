Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q35 · PDF p7

The major Amaravati stupa tradition is associated especially with patronage under which dynasty?

Answer: A. Satavahanas

Amaravati is a major early Buddhist site associated with the Satavahana period. Do not place it in a medieval dynasty just because a later monument is more familiar.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q111 · PDF p23

Who succeeded Guru Nanak as the second Sikh Guru?

Answer: C. Guru Angad

Guru Angad succeeded Guru Nanak. The other Gurus belonged to later stages; chronology matters more than name familiarity.

किसी बात की इच्छा/आकांक्षा से रहित व्यक्ति के लिए उपयुक्त शब्द क्या है?
