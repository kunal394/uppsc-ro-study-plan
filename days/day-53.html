Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q106 · PDF p22

Which listed pulse is generally a kharif crop rather than a rabi pulse?

Answer: A. Pigeon pea

Pigeon pea is generally grown as a kharif pulse. Chickpea and lentil are key rabi pulses. Local cropping variations do not erase the standard seasonal classification.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q103 · PDF p22

General yellowing with poor plant growth commonly indicates deficiency of which major nutrient?

Answer: D. Nitrogen

Nitrogen deficiency commonly causes pale/yellow leaves and reduced growth. Symptom pattern and leaf age matter in real diagnosis; this is the general exam-level association.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q138 · PDF p28

Biofertilisers chiefly consist of what?

Answer: B. Useful microorganism cultures

Biofertilisers contain beneficial microorganisms that improve nutrient availability, such as nitrogen-fixing organisms. They are not interchangeable with all organic manure.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q39 · PDF p8

Hydroponics grows plants primarily using what arrangement?

Answer: A. A nutrient solution without soil

Hydroponics supplies nutrients through a solution rather than conventional soil. It is not the same as grafting or simply withholding soil without nutrient provision.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q189 · PDF p33

‘हाथ’ का तत्सम रूप क्या है? Which is the tatsam (Sanskrit-retained) form corresponding to हाथ? The historical word form—not an English synonym—is being tested.

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: B. हस्त

हाथ का तत्सम रूप हस्त है। अग्नि–आग अलग जोड़ी है। प्रश्न तत्सम रूप पहचानने पर केंद्रित करके रूपान्तरित किया गया है।

English explanation and option meanings

हस्त corresponds to हाथ (hand). अग्नि–आग is a different pair meaning fire. The shared English meaning helps comprehension, but does not replace learning the Hindi/Sanskrit pair.

  • A. अग्नि — Fire
  • B. हस्त — Hand
  • C. जल — Water
  • D. मुख — Mouth/face
