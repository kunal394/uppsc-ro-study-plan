The local storms known as Nor'westers are associated particularly with which region?

Answer: A. Bengal and Assam

Nor'westers/Kalbaisakhi are pre-monsoon storms associated with Bengal and adjoining northeastern areas. They can influence tea/rice cultivation.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q12 · PDF p3

The distance at which one astronomical unit subtends one arcsecond is called what?

Answer: D. A parsec

A parsec is defined by this parallax geometry. A light-year is defined using the distance light travels in a year; the two are not identical.
