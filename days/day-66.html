Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q38 · PDF p8

Which acid is characteristically present in vinegar?

Answer: C. Acetic

Vinegar contains acetic acid. Sour milk is linked with lactic acid, tamarind with tartaric acid, and nettle sting with methanoic acid in the familiar textbook matching exercise.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q37 · PDF p8

Photosynthesis chiefly converts light energy into which stored form?

Answer: A. Chemical energy in organic food

Plants use light energy to form organic compounds, storing chemical energy. Sugar may later be converted into starch; that conversion does not itself explain every step of light-energy capture.

Which brain region is called the 'little brain'?

Answer: D. Cerebellum

Cerebellum literally denotes the little brain and helps coordinate movement, balance and posture. The cerebrum has different major functions.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q158 · PDF p30

इनमें ‘वृक्ष’ का पर्यायवाची कौन-सा नहीं है? Which word is NOT a synonym of वृक्ष (tree)?

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: C. विशिख

पादप, तरु और द्रुम वृक्ष से जुड़े पर्याय हैं। विशिख सामान्यतः बाण/तीर को बताता है; अर्थ देखकर अपवाद पहचानें।

English explanation and option meanings

पादप, तरु and द्रुम are associated with वृक्ष. विशिख ordinarily means arrow, making it the exception. Option meanings remain hidden until reveal.

  • A. पादप — Plant/tree
  • B. तरु — Tree
  • C. विशिख — Arrow
  • D. द्रुम — Tree

‘नदी’ के लिए उपयुक्त पर्याय चुनें। Choose an appropriate synonym for नदी (river).

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: A. तटिनी

तटिनी नदी का पर्याय है। वेणी सामान्यतः गुँथे बाल/चोटी का बोध कराती है। शब्द का संदर्भ भी ध्यान में रखें।

English explanation and option meanings

तटिनी means river; वेणी usually refers to braided hair. Check context as well as the dictionary meaning.

  • B. पर्वत — Mountain
  • C. वेणी — Braid/plait
  • D. शिखर — Peak
