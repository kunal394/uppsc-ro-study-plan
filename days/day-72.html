Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q115 · PDF p24

Which constitutional institution recommends how specified tax proceeds should be shared between the Union and states?

Answer: B. Finance Commission

The Finance Commission's Article 280 role includes recommendations on distribution of net tax proceeds and grants. It recommends; it does not itself legislate the tax.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q117 · PDF p24

Which body produces India's official national-income estimates in the current statistical framework?

Answer: C. National Statistical Office

National-income estimates are produced by the National Statistical Office under the statistics ministry. The RBI performs monetary/banking functions; the Finance Commission addresses fiscal transfers.

Nivesh Mitra is associated with which state government?

Nivesh Mitra is Uttar Pradesh's investor-facilitation/single-window system. Associate the initiative with purpose as well as state.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q148 · PDF p29

‘अभिज्ञ’ का विपरीत अर्थ वाला शब्द चुनें। Choose the word with the opposite meaning to अभिज्ञ.

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: A. अनभिज्ञ

अभिज्ञ का अर्थ जानकार/परिचित है; अनभिज्ञ का अर्थ जानकारी न रखने वाला है। सर्वज्ञ और विज्ञ विपरीत अर्थ नहीं देते।

English explanation and option meanings

अभिज्ञ means informed or acquainted; अनभिज्ञ means unaware. सर्वज्ञ and विज्ञ do not express the opposite. Meanings are shown only after reveal so the gloss does not answer the question in advance.

  • A. अनभिज्ञ — Unaware/unacquainted
  • B. सर्वज्ञ — All-knowing
  • C. विज्ञ — Informed/knowledgeable
  • D. ज्ञानी — Learned/wise

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q150 · PDF p29

‘सूक्ष्म’ के विपरीत अर्थ की सही जोड़ी कौन-सी है? Which pair gives the correct opposite meaning of सूक्ष्म?

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: C. सूक्ष्म–स्थूल

सूक्ष्म का विलोम स्थूल है। अर्थ का आकार/बारीकी वाला पक्ष देखें; कोमल और लघु समान विलोम नहीं हैं।

English explanation and option meanings

स्थूल is the opposite of सूक्ष्म in the fine/subtle versus gross/coarse sense. कोमल (soft) and लघु (small) are different contrasts.

  • A. सूक्ष्म–कोमल — Fine/subtle – soft
  • B. सूक्ष्म–लघु — Fine/subtle – small
  • C. सूक्ष्म–स्थूल — Fine/subtle – gross/coarse
  • D. सूक्ष्म–सूचना — Fine/subtle – information
