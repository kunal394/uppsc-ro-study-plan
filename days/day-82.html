Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q53 · PDF p12

In which part of the Constitution are the provisions on elections grouped?

Answer: C. Part XV

Part XV covers elections, including the Election Commission framework. Fundamental Rights are in Part III; emergency provisions are in Part XVIII.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q55 · PDF p12

Articles 23 and 24 primarily protect which fundamental right?

Answer: D. Protection against exploitation

Article 23 prohibits trafficking and forced labour; Article 24 concerns child employment in specified hazardous settings. Articles 19–22 concern freedoms/protections, not this pair.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q115 · PDF p24

Which constitutional institution recommends how specified tax proceeds should be shared between the Union and states?

Answer: B. Finance Commission

The Finance Commission's Article 280 role includes recommendations on distribution of net tax proceeds and grants. It recommends; it does not itself legislate the tax.

The description 'quasi-federal' for India's constitutional system is associated with which scholar?

Answer: A. K. C. Wheare

K. C. Wheare used this description to emphasise the strong central features alongside federal arrangements. Do not confuse this attribution with a constitutional article.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q26 · PDF p6

A bag has ₹5, ₹2 and ₹1 coins in a 3:4:5 count ratio, worth ₹2,100 in total. How many ₹5 coins are there?

Answer: D. 225

Let counts be 3x, 4x and 5x. Value is 15x + 8x + 5x = 28x = 2100, so x = 75 and the ₹5-coin count is 225. Do not confuse value ratio with count ratio.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q170 · PDF p31

‘चरित्रवान व्यक्ति’ में ‘चरित्रवान’ किस प्रकार का विशेषण है? In चरित्रवान व्यक्ति, which Hindi adjective category describes चरित्रवान?

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: D. गुणवाचक

चरित्रवान व्यक्ति का गुण बताता है, संख्या या मात्रा नहीं। इसलिए यह गुणवाचक विशेषण है।

English explanation and option meanings

चरित्रवान व्यक्ति means a person of good character. चरित्रवान expresses a quality, not number or amount, so it is गुणवाचक. English labels are learning glosses for Hindi grammar categories.

  • A. संख्यावाचक — Numeral adjective
  • B. परिमाणवाचक — Quantity adjective
  • C. सार्वनामिक — Pronominal adjective
  • D. गुणवाचक — Quality adjective

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q152 · PDF p30

‘कुछ लोग आए’ में ‘कुछ’ का विशेषण-भेद क्या है? In कुछ लोग आए, what type of Hindi adjective is कुछ?

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: A. अनिश्चित संख्यावाचक

‘लोग’ गिने जा सकते हैं लेकिन संख्या निश्चित नहीं है, इसलिए ‘कुछ’ अनिश्चित संख्यावाचक है। ‘कुछ पानी’ में मात्रा होने से परिमाण का प्रसंग अलग होगा। स्कैन का हाथ से चिह्नित विकल्प इस नियम का प्रमाण नहीं है।

English explanation and option meanings

कुछ लोग आए means ‘some people came’. People are countable, but the number is unspecified, so कुछ is an indefinite numeral adjective. कुछ पानी means ‘some water’ and concerns amount instead. A handwritten mark is not evidence for this grammar rule.

  • A. अनिश्चित संख्यावाचक — Indefinite numeral
  • B. परिमाणवाचक — Quantity adjective
  • C. गुणवाचक — Quality adjective
  • D. निश्चित संख्यावाचक — Definite numeral
