Haldighati was fought in 1576. 1526 and 1556 refer to the first and second battles of Panipat; 1761 to the third.

What is the administrative third volume of Abu'l Fazl's Akbarnama known as?

Answer: B. Ain-i-Akbari

Ain-i-Akbari records the empire's administrative arrangements. Keep author, ruler and text together; Baburnama and Humayunnama are different works.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q134 · PDF p28

An urban heat island means what?

Answer: D. Urban areas are warmer than nearby rural areas

Built surfaces, less vegetation and waste heat can raise urban temperatures relative to nearby rural surroundings. This is a relative-temperature effect, not a claim that every city is always hotter.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q20 · PDF p4

The ecological ten-percent energy-transfer rule is associated with whom?

Answer: C. Raymond Lindeman

Lindeman is associated with the ten-percent rule: only a small fraction of energy passes to the next trophic level. Treat 10% as the simplified model, not an exact constant for every ecosystem.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q173 · PDF p31

किसी बात की इच्छा/आकांक्षा से रहित व्यक्ति के लिए उपयुक्त शब्द क्या है? Choose one Hindi word for a person free from desire or longing.

English option meanings stay hidden until reveal: translating the options first could give away the answer. English is support; the Hindi form/meaning is the skill being practised.

उत्तर: B. निःस्पृह

निःस्पृह का अर्थ इच्छा/स्पृहा से रहित है। निर्भीक भय का अभाव बताता है, जिज्ञासु जानने की इच्छा रखता है।

English explanation and option meanings

निःस्पृह means free from desire; निर्भीक means fearless, while जिज्ञासु expresses a wish to know. These are different states.

  • A. निर्भीक — Fearless
  • B. निःस्पृह — Free from desire
  • C. जिज्ञासु — Curious / eager to know
  • D. वाचाल — Talkative
