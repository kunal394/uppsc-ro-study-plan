Mains after prelims

Keep prelims 90–95% of this cycle. The optional weekly bridge is capped at 30 minutes inside recall, not extra daily essays or a parallel descriptive syllabus. After prelims, use the four focused guides below and a separate timed-writing cycle.

Editorial post-prelims progression

  • First: diagnose descriptive gaps—post-independence to 1965, society, governance/ethics and official Hindi drafting.
  • Then: make one-page topic frameworks, practise short answers, receive/check specific corrections, and rewrite weak answers.
  • Next: practise paired 75/125-word answers and separate drafting/translation exercises; build essay outlines before complete essays.
  • Finally: use timed full-paper writing with the notified structure and review relevance, organisation, accuracy, word limits and time. This is a scalable roadmap, not an invented exam-date countdown.
