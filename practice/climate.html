Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q82 · PDF p17

The local storms known as Nor'westers are associated particularly with which region?

Answer: A. Bengal and Assam

Nor'westers/Kalbaisakhi are pre-monsoon storms associated with Bengal and adjoining northeastern areas. They can influence tea/rice cultivation.
