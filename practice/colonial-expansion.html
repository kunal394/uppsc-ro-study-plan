Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q90 · PDF p19

Which of these places was annexed under Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse?

Answer: A. Jhansi

Jhansi was a Doctrine of Lapse annexation. Punjab and Awadh were acquired through different grounds/events; do not classify every British annexation as lapse.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q124 · PDF p26

Permanent Settlement is most directly associated with which region?

Answer: C. Bengal

Permanent Settlement was introduced in Bengal in 1793. Ryotwari is associated with major parts of Madras/Bombay; Mahalwari with parts of northern India.
