Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q23 · PDF p5

Who was the first woman to preside over the Indian National Congress, distinct from its first Indian woman president?

Answer: C. Annie Besant

Annie Besant presided in 1917. Sarojini Naidu, in 1925, was the first Indian woman president. This distinction is the trap, not simply recognition of a famous name.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q91 · PDF p19

Who was Viceroy when the Indian National Congress was founded in 1885?

Answer: D. Lord Dufferin

Lord Dufferin's tenure included 1885. Ripon preceded him; Curzon served later, while Wellesley belonged to the earlier Governor-General era.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q93 · PDF p19

Where did the first Indian National Congress session meet in 1885?

Answer: B. Bombay

The first session met in Bombay under W. C. Bonnerjee. City, year and president should be recalled as a linked set.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q119 · PDF p25

Which Congress session adopted the Purna Swaraj goal in 1929?

Answer: D. Lahore

The Lahore session of December 1929, presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, adopted Purna Swaraj. The independence observance followed on 26 January 1930.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q120 · PDF p25

Whom did Subhas Chandra Bose defeat in the 1939 Congress presidential election?

Answer: B. Pattabhi Sitaramayya

Bose defeated Pattabhi Sitaramayya in 1939. Distinguish the election from Bose's earlier 1938 Haripura presidency.
