Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q94 · PDF p20

Which brain region is called the 'little brain'?

Answer: D. Cerebellum

Cerebellum literally denotes the little brain and helps coordinate movement, balance and posture. The cerebrum has different major functions.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q98 · PDF p20

Which pair contains water-soluble vitamins?

Answer: C. B-group and C

B-group vitamins and vitamin C are water-soluble. A, D, E and K are classified as fat-soluble.
