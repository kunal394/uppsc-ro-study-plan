Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q22 · PDF p5

Choose the correct sequence from earliest to latest.

Answer: B. Cripps → Quit India → Cabinet Mission → Mountbatten Plan

Cripps Mission: March 1942; Quit India launch: August 1942; Cabinet Mission: 1946; Mountbatten Plan: June 1947. Attach each date to its purpose.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q120 · PDF p25

Whom did Subhas Chandra Bose defeat in the 1939 Congress presidential election?

Answer: B. Pattabhi Sitaramayya

Bose defeated Pattabhi Sitaramayya in 1939. Distinguish the election from Bose's earlier 1938 Haripura presidency.
