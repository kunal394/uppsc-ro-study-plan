Aqua regia combines concentrated nitric acid with which acid?

Answer: B. Hydrochloric acid

Aqua regia is a mixture of concentrated HCl and HNO₃, conventionally 3:1 by volume. This is a chemistry fact, not an instruction to prepare or handle it.
