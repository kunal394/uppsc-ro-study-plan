Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q26 · PDF p6

A bag has ₹5, ₹2 and ₹1 coins in a 3:4:5 count ratio, worth ₹2,100 in total. How many ₹5 coins are there?

Answer: D. 225

Let counts be 3x, 4x and 5x. Value is 15x + 8x + 5x = 28x = 2100, so x = 75 and the ₹5-coin count is 225. Do not confuse value ratio with count ratio.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q28 · PDF p6

A is seventh from the left, B fourth from the right. After swapping, A is fifteenth from the left. What is B's new position from the right?

Answer: C. Twelfth

B's original left position was 15, so total = 15 + 4 − 1 = 18. B moves to left position 7; from the right it is 18 − 7 + 1 = 12.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q57 · PDF p13

A person leaves at 6:40 AM, travels for 25 minutes, then spends 15 minutes at breakfast. When does the next journey start?

Answer: B. 7:20 AM

6:40 + 25 minutes = 7:05; another 15 minutes gives 7:20. Time for the later journey does not enter the departure time asked.
