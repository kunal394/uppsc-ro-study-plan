Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q52 · PDF p12

Which constitutional provision specifically prohibits discrimination on listed grounds in public employment?

Answer: B. Article 16(2)

Article 16 concerns equality of opportunity in public employment. Clause (2) lists prohibited grounds; Article 15 concerns a different general discrimination provision.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q55 · PDF p12

Articles 23 and 24 primarily protect which fundamental right?

Answer: D. Protection against exploitation

Article 23 prohibits trafficking and forced labour; Article 24 concerns child employment in specified hazardous settings. Articles 19–22 concern freedoms/protections, not this pair.
