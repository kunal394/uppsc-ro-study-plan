Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q64 · PDF p14

Which Indian initiative promotes environment-friendly everyday lifestyles?

Answer: D. Mission LiFE

LiFE means Lifestyle for Environment. Its focus is sustainable everyday behaviour, not a single satellite or banking system.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q65 · PDF p14

After Chandrayaan-3's successful 2023 soft landing, India became which country in the sequence of nations achieving a lunar soft landing?

Answer: C. Fourth

India became the fourth after the Soviet Union, United States and China. This is the historical 2023 milestone, not a prediction about later missions.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q2 · PDF p2

The 2024 Medicine Nobel for Victor Ambros and Gary Ruvkun recognised work on what?

Answer: A. microRNA and gene regulation

The 2024 prize recognised microRNA and its role in post-transcriptional gene regulation. Keep the award year attached to the discovery/topic.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q58 · PDF p13

Which two players contested the 2024 World Chess Championship match?

Answer: D. Ding Liren and D. Gukesh

Ding Liren and D. Gukesh contested the 2024 title match. Do not replace a historical match pair with later rankings or champions.
