Which monument is the monumental Buddhist temple/stupa complex in Java, Indonesia?

Answer: B. Borobudur

Borobudur is in central Java, Indonesia. The other named sites are in India. This adaptation tests location rather than an ambiguous size comparison.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q41 · PDF p9

Which strait separates Tasmania from mainland Australia?

Answer: A. Bass Strait

Bass Strait separates Tasmania and mainland Australia. Palk Strait lies between India and Sri Lanka; Bering links the Arctic/Pacific region.

The Wardha Valley coalfield belongs to which state?

Wardha Valley coalfields are in Maharashtra. Talcher is in Odisha and Korba in Chhattisgarh; use a resource map to separate the names.

The local storms known as Nor'westers are associated particularly with which region?

Answer: A. Bengal and Assam

Nor'westers/Kalbaisakhi are pre-monsoon storms associated with Bengal and adjoining northeastern areas. They can influence tea/rice cultivation.
