Choose the correct sequence from earliest to latest.

Answer: B. Cripps → Quit India → Cabinet Mission → Mountbatten Plan

Cripps Mission: March 1942; Quit India launch: August 1942; Cabinet Mission: 1946; Mountbatten Plan: June 1947. Attach each date to its purpose.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q23 · PDF p5

Who was the first woman to preside over the Indian National Congress, distinct from its first Indian woman president?

Answer: C. Annie Besant

Annie Besant presided in 1917. Sarojini Naidu, in 1925, was the first Indian woman president. This distinction is the trap, not simply recognition of a famous name.

Which of these places was annexed under Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse?

Answer: A. Jhansi

Jhansi was a Doctrine of Lapse annexation. Punjab and Awadh were acquired through different grounds/events; do not classify every British annexation as lapse.

Who was Viceroy when the Indian National Congress was founded in 1885?

Lord Dufferin's tenure included 1885. Ripon preceded him; Curzon served later, while Wellesley belonged to the earlier Governor-General era.

The major Amaravati stupa tradition is associated especially with patronage under which dynasty?

Answer: A. Satavahanas

Amaravati is a major early Buddhist site associated with the Satavahana period. Do not place it in a medieval dynasty just because a later monument is more familiar.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q105 · PDF p22

Which collection in the Buddhist Tripitaka contains discourses/teachings?

Answer: D. Sutta Pitaka

Sutta Pitaka contains discourses. Vinaya concerns monastic discipline; Abhidhamma deals with analytical/doctrinal matters. Mahavamsa is a chronicle, not the Sutta collection.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q108 · PDF p23

What is the administrative third volume of Abu'l Fazl's Akbarnama known as?

Answer: B. Ain-i-Akbari

Ain-i-Akbari records the empire's administrative arrangements. Keep author, ruler and text together; Baburnama and Humayunnama are different works.
