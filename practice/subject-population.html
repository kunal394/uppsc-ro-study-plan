Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q19 · PDF p4

According to Census 2011, which Indian state had the highest population density?

Answer: B. Bihar

Bihar had the highest density among states in Census 2011. UP's large total population does not mean the highest density; Union Territories are a separate comparison.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q134 · PDF p28

An urban heat island means what?

Answer: D. Urban areas are warmer than nearby rural areas

Built surfaces, less vegetation and waste heat can raise urban temperatures relative to nearby rural surroundings. This is a relative-temperature effect, not a claim that every city is always hotter.

Which of these Union Territories had the highest literacy rate in Census 2011?

Answer: C. Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep had the highest literacy rate among these choices in Census 2011. Literacy rate is a percentage, not total literate population.
