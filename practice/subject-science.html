Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q103 · PDF p22

General yellowing with poor plant growth commonly indicates deficiency of which major nutrient?

Answer: D. Nitrogen

Nitrogen deficiency commonly causes pale/yellow leaves and reduced growth. Symptom pattern and leaf age matter in real diagnosis; this is the general exam-level association.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q9 · PDF p3

How many nanometres make one metre?

Answer: B. 10⁹

Nano means 10⁻⁹ of a unit, so one metre is 10⁹ nanometres. Convert the prefix instead of memorising a large number blindly.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q11 · PDF p3

A reflector intended to send a near-parallel headlight beam commonly uses which mirror?

Answer: A. Concave

A light source near a concave mirror's focus gives a near-parallel reflected beam. Convex mirrors are commonly used for wider rear-view fields.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q12 · PDF p3

The distance at which one astronomical unit subtends one arcsecond is called what?

Answer: D. A parsec

A parsec is defined by this parallax geometry. A light-year is defined using the distance light travels in a year; the two are not identical.

Aqua regia combines concentrated nitric acid with which acid?

Answer: B. Hydrochloric acid

Aqua regia is a mixture of concentrated HCl and HNO₃, conventionally 3:1 by volume. This is a chemistry fact, not an instruction to prepare or handle it.

Which acid is characteristically present in vinegar?

Vinegar contains acetic acid. Sour milk is linked with lactic acid, tamarind with tartaric acid, and nettle sting with methanoic acid in the familiar textbook matching exercise.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q37 · PDF p8

Photosynthesis chiefly converts light energy into which stored form?

Answer: A. Chemical energy in organic food

Plants use light energy to form organic compounds, storing chemical energy. Sugar may later be converted into starch; that conversion does not itself explain every step of light-energy capture.

A conventional Ethernet MAC address has how many bits?

Answer: D. 48

The standard EUI-48/MAC address used in conventional Ethernet has 48 bits, typically displayed as six octets. IP address sizes are a different concept.

After Chandrayaan-3's successful 2023 soft landing, India became which country in the sequence of nations achieving a lunar soft landing?

Answer: C. Fourth

India became the fourth after the Soviet Union, United States and China. This is the historical 2023 milestone, not a prediction about later missions.
