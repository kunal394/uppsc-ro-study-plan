Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q92 · PDF p19

The Central Hindu College in Banaras, later linked with BHU's development, was founded by whom?

Answer: A. Annie Besant

Annie Besant established Central Hindu College in 1898. Madan Mohan Malaviya's central role in BHU's establishment is a related but different fact.

Which listed crop is a major commercial crop of Uttar Pradesh?

Answer: B. Sugarcane

Sugarcane is a major commercial crop of UP, closely linked with sugar industries. The alternatives have different dominant climatic/regional requirements.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q107 · PDF p23

Nivesh Mitra is associated with which state government?

Answer: B. Uttar Pradesh

Nivesh Mitra is Uttar Pradesh's investor-facilitation/single-window system. Associate the initiative with purpose as well as state.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q77 · PDF p16

UP's Operation Kayakalp is chiefly associated with improving what?

Answer: A. School infrastructure/basic education facilities

Operation Kayakalp focuses on improving facilities in basic education schools. It should not be confused with university expansion or an unrelated scheme.
