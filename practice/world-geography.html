Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q41 · PDF p9

Which strait separates Tasmania from mainland Australia?

Answer: A. Bass Strait

Bass Strait separates Tasmania and mainland Australia. Palk Strait lies between India and Sri Lanka; Bering links the Arctic/Pacific region.

Which Indian port is a standard example of an artificial harbour?

Answer: D. Chennai

Chennai is an artificial harbour. Visakhapatnam and Mumbai are commonly studied as natural-harbour examples.

Selected adapted PYQ · 2025 · Q129 · PDF p27

Benguela is a current of which ocean?

Answer: D. Atlantic

The cold Benguela Current flows along southwestern Africa in the Atlantic. California and Humboldt are Pacific currents.
