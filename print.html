Day 1: Diagnostic, not a judgement

Foundation · Diagnostic

0/5

Full-paper day: 180-minute unseen attempt + 60-minute triage. With 2 hours/day, split and shift the cycle; do not call it a 3-hour simulation.

Attempt the 2021 GS and Hindi papers together without looking at solutions. Set a 180-minute timer and use a numbered answer sheet. If 3 uninterrupted hours are not available, split the attempt into two sessions and label it untimed/split, not exam simulation.

The embedded 8 cards are not a 200-question mock. A genuine simulation needs an unseen, unmarked 200-question paper from a lawful source you already use. Otherwise use a shorter learning drill, without reporting a mock score.
