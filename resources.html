Study materials: selective and lawful

Start with free NCERT and official sources; use paid books only if needed. One source per subject is enough. Topic pages name the actual sections to read, so this library is not a demand to buy/read everything. Existing editions work for static concepts; verify changing law, rates, budgets and schemes officially.

Official · free

NCERT textbooks: official free portal (opens a new tab)

Select class, subject and book, then the named chapter. Use Hindi or English for GS. Start with classes 6–10 only where foundations are weak; use class 11 Physical Geography / India: Physical Environment, class 12 India: People and Economy, class 11 Indian Economic Development and class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics selectively. New editions may rename chapters: match the topic, not a guessed page number. Do not read every book cover to cover.

Publisher catalogue · paid optional

M. Laxmikanth: Indian Polity (opens a new tab)

Read only the named topics for each day: Constitution, rights, institutions, federalism, bodies and local government. Your existing edition is adequate for static concepts; verify changes in law officially. Free alternative: NCERT political science plus the Constitution below. No paid course is necessary.

Official · free

Constitution of India (opens a new tab)

Use the current English or Hindi text to verify articles, rights and schedules. Read the relevant provision after attempting a polity question; do not try to memorise the whole document.

Publisher catalogue · paid optional

Spectrum: A Brief History of Modern India (opens a new tab)

One reference for British expansion, reform, nationalism and movements. Read selected sections named in the plan. Free foundation: NCERT history, especially class 8 Our Pasts III / equivalent colonial-history chapters. Avoid a second modern-history reference.

Publisher catalogue · paid optional

वासुदेवनन्दन प्रसाद: आधुनिक हिन्दी व्याकरण और रचना (opens a new tab)

एक ही व्याकरण पुस्तक पर्याप्त है। विलोम, पर्यायवाची, तत्सम–तद्भव, विशेष्य–विशेषण, वाक्यांश और अशुद्धि-शोधन वाले अंश पढ़ें। रोज नियम समझकर उदाहरण लिखें; पूरी पुस्तक एक साथ समाप्त करना लक्ष्य नहीं है। निःशुल्क शुरुआत: नीचे दिए वास्तविक हिन्दी प्रश्नपत्र और NCERT हिन्दी की पठनीय सामग्री; संदेह होने पर विश्वसनीय व्याकरण/शब्दकोश से पुष्टि करें।

Publisher catalogue · paid optional

Arihant: Uttar Pradesh GK / RO–ARO catalogue (opens a new tab)

Choose just one UP GK book (Hindi or English) and, if needed, one solved RO/ARO PYQ compilation. The catalogue also contains old-pattern practice sets: check the actual 200-question combined pattern before buying a mock. Use UP tourism, budget, Census and government sources below as free alternatives and updates.

Third-party study material · free archive

Drishti: UP monthly current-affairs archive (opens a new tab)

Choose one monthly compilation, not several coaching feeds. Read the latest completed month during daily sessions and work backwards through your backlog. Also cover national/international news through PIB. A rolling 6–12-month window is a planning starting point, not an official cutoff; adjust when the exam date is announced. Old paper current affairs are historical, not current facts.

Official · free

PIB: national developments and schemes (opens a new tab)

For selected developments, note what changed, ministry/institution, objective, eligibility, location and one static connection. Do not copy press releases or read every release.

Official · free

Union Budget and Economic Survey (opens a new tab)

Use the latest available Budget at a Glance / highlights and Survey chapter summaries. Understand deficit, spending, sectors and agriculture first. Date all numerical notes; do not memorise the entire Survey.

Official · free

Uttar Pradesh budget portal (opens a new tab)

Open the latest state budget documents. Record major schemes and sector priorities with year, units and source page in your own notebook. Separate actual expenditure, revised estimates and budget estimates.

Official · free

Reserve Bank of India (opens a new tab)

Verify monetary-policy terms and current rates from the latest policy statement. Focus on inflation, repo rate, CRR, banking and foreign-exchange basics; older PYQ values must keep their historical date.

Official · free

Census of India (opens a new tab)

Use the year explicitly asked in the paper (many questions use Census 2011). Compare density, literacy, sex ratio, urbanisation and growth; a percentage is not an absolute count. Verify the latest release status rather than assuming a later census dataset exists.

Official · free

UP Tourism: culture and places (opens a new tab)

Build district–place–river–tradition associations for UP. Focus on cultural circuits, heritage, religious sites and crafts; tourism descriptions are not a substitute for the whole history syllabus.

Official · free

Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (opens a new tab)

Use published statistics and scheme documents to verify crop seasons, production years, MSP and farmer support. Understand concepts before memorising rankings.

Official · free

NDMA: disaster-management resources (opens a new tab)

Read the relevant hazard guidance: flood, drought, earthquake and heat wave. Know mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery; link UP floods and Bundelkhand drought to geography.

Official · free

ISRO: missions and applications (opens a new tab)

Read mission overviews, launch-vehicle basics and remote-sensing applications. Note objective, agency and application, not engineering-level specifications.

Official · free

Rajbhasha: official Hindi dictionary resources (opens a new tab)

For the later mains Hindi roadmap, use official dictionary and administrative-language resources. Practise meanings in context and both translation directions; use the matching commercial-terminology section in your Hindi text as well. This portal is a terminology aid, not a complete mains textbook.
