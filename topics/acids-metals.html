Understand before memorising

pH describes acidity/basicity in the studied aqueous context; learn the scale and indicator behaviour rather than only one natural-source pair. Neutralisation connects acid/base chemistry with salt formation.

Corrosion is a chemical process affected by conditions such as oxygen and moisture. Alloys and protective coatings have different purposes/mechanisms. This is conceptual exam study, not instructions for hazardous experiments.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Use a labelled pH scale to locate acidic, neutral and basic examples. Compare painting iron with making an alloy: both may affect durability, but by different mechanisms.
