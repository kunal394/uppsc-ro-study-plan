Understand before memorising

A budget records different categories of receipts and expenditure. A deficit is a defined difference, not simply 'government has no money'. Learn the definition before interpreting a headline number.

The current account and foreign-exchange reserves are different measures. A stock of reserves is not the same as annual exports or a current-account balance. Distinguish actuals, revised estimates and budget estimates.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Create three columns for actuals, revised estimates and budget estimates for the same spending head. A changed estimate is not automatically an observed change in spending.
