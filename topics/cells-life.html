Understand before memorising

Cells perform functions through specialised structures; tissues organise cells for shared functions. Blood is connective tissue, not merely a red liquid to memorise in a list.

Photosynthesis stores chemical energy in organic food; respiration releases usable energy from food. Breathing moves air, while cellular respiration is a biochemical process. Learn the input–site–output–purpose of each process.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw two chains: light + raw materials → food formation, and food breakdown → energy release. A plant carries out respiration too; do not equate plant with photosynthesis and animal with respiration exclusively.
