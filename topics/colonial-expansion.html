British expansion and land revenue

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • European trading companies
  • Plassey and Buxar

  • Doctrine of Lapse
  • Awadh annexation
  • Permanent, Ryotwari and Mahalwari systems

Understand before memorising

Trace how commercial footholds became territorial and fiscal power. Military victory, a revenue right and a formal annexation are different changes; attach each event to its consequence.

Subsidiary Alliance, Doctrine of Lapse and annexation on alleged misgovernment were different instruments. Revenue systems differed in the entity assessed, regional use and effects on cultivators; do not assign all British acquisitions to Dalhousie's lapse policy.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare Jhansi and Awadh: both entered British control, but the stated grounds differed. Then compare settlement with zamindars, individual cultivators and village-based units in a four-column table.
