Understand before memorising

Use sessions as anchors in a political story rather than an isolated list of presidents. The first woman president and first Indian woman president are different facts.

Swadeshi involved economic and political mobilisation: boycott, indigenous production and public organisation. Dates of announcements, protests and implementation can differ, so identify which event a question asks about.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Build a timeline from 1885 through partition and Surat. Add one column for political method and one for the effect on participation or organisation.
