Constitution: making, features and Preamble

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Constituent Assembly and constitution-making
  • Adoption versus commencement
  • Written Constitution and constitutional supremacy
  • Parliamentary government and federal features
  • Preamble: sovereignty, socialism, secularism, democracy, republic
  • Justice, liberty, equality and fraternity

Understand before memorising

A constitution sets the rules under which ordinary laws and governments operate. Learn who creates an institution, what it may do, and how it can be held accountable; a list of dates alone does not explain constitutional government.

Adoption on 26 November 1949 and general commencement on 26 January 1950 are different events. In a parliamentary system the executive is accountable to the legislature; federal arrangements divide powers between levels, while a strong Union gives India's system distinctive central features.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

An elected legislature passes a law. That does not by itself make the law constitutional: courts can examine it against constitutional limits. Separate democratic election, legislative competence and protection of rights in your explanation.
