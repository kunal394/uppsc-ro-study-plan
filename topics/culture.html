Understand before memorising

Culture questions often test an association, but preparation should explain the tradition's region, period and function. Do not confuse a classical form, a folk performance, a religious practice and a craft.

Compare teachings and institutions without assuming all traditions are identical. For sites, use place–style–period–patron links; for literature, use author–work–language/context.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Create four small maps/tables: teachers, monuments, performances and crafts. Give each entry a region and a distinctive feature instead of collecting only names.
