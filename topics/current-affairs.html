Understand before memorising

Current-affairs preparation is a dated, selective record, not an endless feed. For each item learn what changed, the responsible institution, its purpose, and a static concept connecting it to the syllabus.

Keep historical PYQ answers separate from current notes. A 2024 award or a Census 2011 fact should not be silently updated because a newer result exists. A rolling 6–12-month starting window is editorial planning, not an official limit.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

For a science award, write award year, recipients, recognised discovery and the relevant biology concept. For a scheme, replace the discovery column with objective and beneficiary.

Closed-book learning objectives

Completion means you can do these without copying the text. Explain in your own words, check against the source and repair gaps.

  • Produce at most five dated notes per normal day
  • Explain each item's static connection
  • Keep historical answers and current facts distinct
