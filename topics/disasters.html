Understand before memorising

A hazard becomes a disaster through exposure and vulnerability. The same rainfall event can have very different effects depending on settlement, drainage, preparedness and infrastructure.

Mitigation reduces underlying risk; preparedness plans action before an event; response addresses immediate needs; recovery concerns restoration and resilience. Link eastern-UP floods and Bundelkhand water stress to physical and social conditions.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

For a fictional flood-prone settlement, separate raising risk-aware planning, advance warnings, immediate evacuation planning and resilient reconstruction into the four management stages.
