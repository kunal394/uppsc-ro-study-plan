DPSPs, duties, citizenship and territory

Understand before memorising

Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties answer different questions. Rights provide protections; DPSPs guide state policy and are not enforceable by a court in the same manner; duties set expectations for citizens.

Citizenship, domicile and residence are not interchangeable. Learn the constitutional framework before reading changing statutory details. Union/territory provisions concern political organisation, not just a memorised state count.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

A welfare-policy aim and a directly enforceable protection may both be constitutionally important, but their enforcement mechanisms differ. Write purpose, beneficiary and enforceability in separate columns.
