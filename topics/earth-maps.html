Maps, Earth and landforms

Expanded editorial learning guide

Understand before memorising

A map is a model: scale changes detail, coordinates fix position, and direction must be anchored to a reference. Learn to draw a simple location rather than rely on recognition of a printed map.

Plate movement explains major structural patterns; weathering, erosion and deposition modify surfaces. Distinguish a process from the landform it produces and a rock's origin from its present location.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Sketch a convergent boundary, then show a river eroding upstream and depositing downstream. Label process → material movement → resulting landform.
