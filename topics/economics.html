National income, sectors and development

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • GDP, GNP/GNI and domestic/national distinction

  • Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors
  • Poverty and unemployment
  • Reforms and structural change
  • National statistical institutions

Understand before memorising

Domestic production and income connected with residents are related but different measures. Nominal growth includes price effects; real measures seek to distinguish changes in output from changes in prices.

Development includes changes in capabilities, opportunities and living conditions, not only a GDP total. Compare sectors by activity, and distinguish output share from employment share.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

If output quantity stays constant but prices rise, nominal value can increase without equivalent real-output growth. Use a two-period quantity–price table to explain the distinction.
