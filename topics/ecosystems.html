Ecosystems, energy and biodiversity

Understand before memorising

Energy flows through trophic levels and is lost as heat, while materials cycle through ecosystems. The simplified ten-percent model is useful for exam reasoning, not an exact universal constant for every transfer.

A food web shows multiple feeding relationships. Biodiversity includes more than the number of large mammals; habitat protection, species protection and conservation-area categories require distinctions.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw grass → herbivore → carnivore, then add an alternate food source and decomposers. Show energy arrows separately from a nutrient-cycle loop.
