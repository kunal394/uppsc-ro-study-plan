Electricity, computers and technology

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Current, voltage, resistance and power
  • Series/parallel circuits
  • Magnetism and induction basics
  • Processor, memory and storage
  • Hardware, applications and operating systems
  • Networks, MAC and IP concepts
  • Space and remote-sensing applications

Understand before memorising

In a circuit, current, potential difference and resistance play different roles. Series and parallel arrangements affect distribution of current/voltage; learn the circuit before memorising appliance behaviour.

Computers combine processing, storage and software. A processor is not an operating system; a MAC address is not an IP address. For space technology, record purpose, agency and application separately from dated mission news.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw two bulbs in series and parallel, marking current paths. Then put processor, storage, operating system and office application in separate boxes in a computer diagram.
