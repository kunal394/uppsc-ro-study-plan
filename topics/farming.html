Understand before memorising

A crop decision combines season, water, temperature, soil and market conditions. Learn a crop–season–region–constraint table rather than assuming the highest producer will be unchanged each year.

Manure, chemical fertiliser and microbial biofertiliser differ in composition and function. A pest, a nutrient deficiency and a disease are different kinds of problem. Farm support, procurement and market prices should not be treated as synonyms.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare sugarcane, wheat and rice for season/water context. Then contrast yellowing associated with a nutrient problem against a named crop disease, using the standard source rather than diagnosing a real crop.
