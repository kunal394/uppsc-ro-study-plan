Understand before memorising

Federalism divides powers, but also requires cooperation. Identify the subject, the competent level, and any relevant constitutional exception before answering a Centre–state question.

The three emergency frameworks have different triggers and consequences. Fiscal transfers involve constitutional recommendations and legal budget processes; the Finance Commission does not itself enact every tax-sharing law.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Separate a law on a listed subject, an emergency proclamation and a recommendation on tax distribution. Each uses a different institutional route even though all concern Union–state relations.
