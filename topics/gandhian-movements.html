Understand before memorising

For each movement learn trigger, social base, method, leadership and outcome. A cause–action–consequence chain is more useful than remembering one slogan.

Movements did not all have identical organisation or timing. Chauri Chaura relates to withdrawal of Non-Cooperation; negotiations and conferences belong in the chronology of later phases.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Place 1917, 1919, 1920, 1930 and 1942 on a blank timeline. For each anchor explain one cause and one consequence, then add the UP connection where relevant.
