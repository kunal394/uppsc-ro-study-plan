Understand before memorising

Heat transfer and temperature are different concepts; conduction, convection and radiation have different mechanisms. In sound, frequency and amplitude explain different properties.

For optics, draw incident and reflected/refracted rays. A luminous body emits visible light, while an illuminated body reflects it. Mirror/lens uses should follow ray behaviour, not just remembered appliance names.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Place a light source near a concave mirror's focus and draw reflected rays. Then compare a convex rear-view mirror's wider field with the headlight reflector's beam purpose.
