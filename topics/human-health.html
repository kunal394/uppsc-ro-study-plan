Understand before memorising

Follow each system's flow rather than memorising isolated organs. In circulation, distinguish chamber, vessel and direction of flow; in coordination distinguish sensing, signalling and response.

Water-soluble B-group/C and fat-soluble A/D/E/K are useful classifications, but also learn functions and deficiency associations. Disease preparation should explain transmission and prevention, not offer diagnosis or treatment advice.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Trace a nutrient from digestion to circulation. Then compare a pathogen, its transmission route and a preventive public-health measure; those are three different kinds of fact.
