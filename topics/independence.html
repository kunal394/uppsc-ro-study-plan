Understand before memorising

Keep different streams of nationalism visible: mass movements, revolutionary activity, worker/peasant politics and negotiations. A single leader's biography is not the entire national movement.

Read proposals by purpose and provisions as well as date. Cripps in 1942, Cabinet Mission in 1946 and Mountbatten Plan in 1947 addressed different stages; do not call every proposal an independence settlement.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw separate political-action and negotiation timelines, then join them at 1942–47. Write one difference between a federation proposal and a partition/transfer plan.
