Understand before memorising

Physiography helps explain drainage, climate, transport and agriculture. First draw India's broad physical regions, then add rivers rather than memorising river lists in isolation.

Source, tributary and mouth answer different questions. Himalayan and peninsular drainage differ in geological setting and flow patterns, but avoid absolute claims that ignore local exceptions.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Trace a river from source through states and tributaries to its mouth. Mark the Ganga–Yamuna confluence and distinguish a river flowing through UP from one merely near its boundary.
