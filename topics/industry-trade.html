Understand before memorising

Industrial location reflects raw materials, labour, energy, transport, markets and policy; one factor rarely explains every industry. A district–product list becomes more useful when you explain those connections.

Output, employment and welfare are different outcomes. Classification thresholds and schemes can change, so retain the year attached to an MSME definition or industrial-policy statistic.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

For a fictional food-processing unit, list raw material, storage, energy, transport and market needs. Compare that with a craft cluster to see why location factors have different weights.
