Understand before memorising

Decentralisation is not just moving a capital closer to residents. It concerns representation, functions, finance and accountability at local levels. Distinguish Gram Sabha participation from an elected Panchayat institution.

The 73rd and 74th amendments provide rural and urban frameworks. Actual devolution depends on state laws and implementation: a Schedule's list should not be mistaken for automatic identical powers everywhere.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

For a village-water issue, list the representative body, participation mechanism, possible funding route and responsible department. Do the same for an urban sanitation issue.
