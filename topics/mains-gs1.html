GS I: descriptive knowledge and answers

Post-prelims descriptive guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Art, culture and history
  • Modern India: 1757–1947
  • Post-independence developments to 1965
  • Society and social issues
  • India/UP geography
  • Environment and disasters
  • Science/technology applications
  • 75-word and 125-word answers

Understand before memorising

After prelims, convert factual knowledge into explanation: cause, consequence, evidence and a concise conclusion. Cover post-independence and society deliberately; the prelims question bank does not cover these descriptive gaps.

A short answer needs direct relevance and prioritisation. A longer answer can add dimensions and a UP example, but still must answer the specific demand rather than reproduce all notes.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

For an original question on urban flooding, connect drainage, land use, vulnerable settlements and governance; finish with practical mitigation. This is an original worked outline, not a past mains question.

Closed-book learning objectives

Completion means you can do these without copying the text. Explain in your own words, check against the source and repair gaps.

  • Cover the descriptive gaps separately
  • Write one relevant 75-word and 125-word answer after prelims
  • Use evidence and a UP example without exceeding the word limit

During the prelims cycle, only an optional ≤30-minute weekly overlap. After prelims, work through the full descriptive checklist and timed answers.
