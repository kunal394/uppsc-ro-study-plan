Understand before memorising

Money is used as medium of exchange, unit of account and store of value. Banking links deposits, lending and payment systems; a central bank has a different institutional role from a commercial bank.

Inflation concerns the price level rather than a single item's temporary increase. Policy tools work through financial conditions and demand, not an automatic identical change in every price. Date every current rate.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare a loan made by a commercial bank with the RBI changing a policy rate. Describe the institutions and the possible transmission chain before memorising a rate number.
