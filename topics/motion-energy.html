Motion, forces, work and energy

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Distance/displacement
  • Speed/velocity/acceleration
  • Force and Newtonian ideas
  • Gravitation and weight
  • Work, energy and power
  • Conservation of energy
  • SI units and prefixes

Understand before memorising

Distance records path length; displacement concerns change of position with direction. Speed and velocity are likewise not identical. Learn each quantity's meaning, unit and relationship before using formulas.

Energy can change form while total energy is conserved in the appropriate system. Power describes rate of work/energy transfer, not a larger synonym for energy.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Walking around a closed square gives non-zero distance but zero final displacement. Explain that in words before calculating; then practise converting kilometre/metre and nano prefixes.
