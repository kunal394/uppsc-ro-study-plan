Mughals, Marathas and regional states

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Mughal ruler chronology
  • Mansabdari and jagirdari
  • Revenue arrangements
  • Akbarnama and Ain-i-Akbari
  • Battles and regional resistance
  • Marathas
  • Awadh and other successor states

Understand before memorising

Distinguish rank, revenue assignment and ownership. Mansab and jagir are related administrative ideas, not synonyms. Study revenue arrangements and political authority together.

Link battles to date, participants and location, and link texts to author and purpose. Ain-i-Akbari is associated with administrative description in Akbarnama; it is not an unrelated ruler's memoir.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare a ruler's rank system with assignment of revenue. Then reconstruct Haldighati's date and participants, and attach Agra/Fatehpur Sikri to the relevant historical context.
