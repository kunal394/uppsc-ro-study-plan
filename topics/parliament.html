Understand before memorising

Bicameralism does not mean both Houses have identical powers. Compare representation, tenure, presiding officer and role in each type of bill. A Money Bill follows a distinct constitutional procedure; do not classify every bill involving expenditure as a Money Bill.

Lawmaking and scrutiny are linked: debates, questions and committees provide mechanisms to examine government action. Learn the path of a bill and then identify where the two Houses' roles differ.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw two flowcharts, one for an ordinary bill and one for a Money Bill. Mark the Speaker's role and the differing Rajya Sabha stage using the constitutional text.
