Understand before memorising

Absolute count, share, density and growth rate are different measures. The most populous state need not have the highest density; a total number of literate people differs from a literacy rate.

Compare figures from the same dataset, year and administrative boundary. Urbanisation involves employment, migration, infrastructure and environmental pressures, not simply more buildings.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Area A has 1,000 people on 10 square units; B has 800 on 4. A has more people, but B is denser: 100 versus 200 per square unit. Label both units before comparing.
