Understand before memorising

Write the operation or relationship before choosing an option. In ranking, convert positions using the total; in direction problems, draw coordinates; in coding, compare repeated symbols or letter positions systematically.

Check a proposed number pattern against every transition, not only the first pair. In relations, infer only what the statement establishes; do not assume gender or a missing family link.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

In a row of 18 people, the person seventh from the left is twelfth from the right: 18 − 7 + 1. Explain the +1 using a short drawn row, then change the total and repeat.
