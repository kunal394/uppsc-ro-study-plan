Reform, education and press

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Brahmo, Arya and Prarthana movements
  • Aligarh movement
  • Theosophical activity
  • Women, education and social reform
  • Colonial education policy
  • Press and public debate
  • Institution–founder–aim comparisons

Understand before memorising

Reform organisations differed in religious approach, social objectives and methods. Build founder–organisation–aim–institution links, then ask how print and education expanded public debate.

Political nationalism and social reform overlapped but were not identical. Distinguish the founder of an earlier college from the founder or organiser of a later university institution.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare two organisations using the same columns. If both supported education, explain the differing aims or constituencies instead of treating that shared word as the entire answer.
