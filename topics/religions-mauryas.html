Understand before memorising

Learn a religious tradition through teaching, organisation, texts and patronage. Buddhist Vinaya, Sutta and Abhidhamma collections are not interchangeable; a chronicle is another type of source.

Mauryan administration and Ashoka's dhamma should be studied alongside inscriptions and regional reach. A religious teaching, a ruler's policy and a later literary account are different categories.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare monastic rules, discourses and philosophical analysis. Then locate Sarnath, Shravasti and Kushinagar and explain their distinct associations.
