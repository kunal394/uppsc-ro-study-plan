Understand before memorising

A resource exists in a geological or ecological setting; production also depends on access, technology and demand. Reserves and annual production are different quantities and may have different rankings.

Soils reflect parent material, climate and processes; vegetation reflects climatic and environmental conditions. Match location and mechanism, not only a crop name.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Create separate maps for soil/vegetation and minerals/energy. Attach Wardha, Korba and Talcher to states, then explain why a production ranking needs a year and unit.
