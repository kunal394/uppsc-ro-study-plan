Understand before memorising

A trigger explains why an event began at a particular moment; structural grievances explain why it spread. Keep both in your notes, instead of reducing the revolt to a single cartridge fact.

Map leaders and centres, then connect participation and coordination problems to outcomes. The 1858 transfer to Crown rule was a consequence, not the date at which the revolt began.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw Meerut, Delhi, Kanpur, Lucknow and Jhansi on a map. For each, write a leader and one local context, then connect the map to a causes–limitations–outcomes sheet.
