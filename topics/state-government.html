Understand before memorising

Use Union institutions as a starting comparison, not a perfect template. A Governor is appointed rather than elected through the President's electoral process. Legislative Councils do not exist in every state.

The Chief Minister and state Council of Ministers are part of a parliamentary executive accountable to the Assembly. Learn constitutional powers and conventions separately; do not substitute a current office-holder list for institutional understanding.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Write President/Governor and PM/CM comparison tables. Add one row saying where the apparent analogy fails, rather than making all entries 'same'.
