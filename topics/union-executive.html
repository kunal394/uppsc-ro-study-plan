Understand before memorising

The President is the constitutional head; the parliamentary executive ordinarily acts through the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers under the constitutional framework. Distinguish an office's formal power from the way it is exercised on advice.

Collective responsibility to Lok Sabha links government survival to legislative confidence. Election, appointment and removal are separate columns: knowing an office-holder's name does not explain the institution.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Compare a President election with appointment of a Prime Minister. Then explain why a government losing legislative confidence faces a different issue from expiry of the President's term.
