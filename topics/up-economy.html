Understand before memorising

UP questions combine local facts with national concepts. Separate a scheme's objective, department, beneficiaries and date. Investment facilitation, school infrastructure and an agricultural input scheme address different problems.

Production rankings, fiscal reports and policy launches are time-sensitive. Record year and units and distinguish a scheme announcement from its implementation or outcomes.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Put Nivesh Mitra, school-facility initiatives and a farm-support measure into purpose–institution–date columns. Then connect one industrial cluster with input and market needs.
