UP regions, rivers and population

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Neighbours and physical regions
  • Terai, plains and southern plateau
  • Rivers and confluences
  • Climate and soils
  • Population and urban centres
  • Irrigation and agricultural constraints
  • Environment and regional vulnerability

Understand before memorising

Build UP knowledge on a map: region → river → rainfall/water availability → crops/settlements. District counts and administrative boundaries may change; verify them before memorising a current number.

Use the same Census year for comparisons. Link Bundelkhand water stress and eastern flood exposure to region-specific conditions, not to a generic statement that the whole state has identical risks.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Draw a three-region UP map and add Ganga, Yamuna, Gomti and Ghaghara. Write one water/cropping constraint for each broad region.
