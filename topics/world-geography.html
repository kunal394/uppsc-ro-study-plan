World locations, oceans and transport

Expanded editorial learning guide

Topic and subtopic breakdown

  • Continents and oceans
  • Warm and cold ocean currents
  • Straits and seas
  • Mountain ranges and passes
  • Plantation regions
  • Natural and artificial harbours
  • Indian transport and trade links

Understand before memorising

Use relationships to remember a place: a strait separates or connects named land/water bodies; a current moves along a coast and influences climate. A disconnected capital list is a low-quality substitute.

Ports combine physical setting with engineered facilities. Distinguish a harbour type from the city name, and a transport corridor from a trade-product ranking.

Original worked example (not a PYQ):

Locate Bass Strait and Benguela Current, then describe their neighbouring land or coast. For Chennai and Mumbai, compare harbour type using the source rather than copying a marked option.
